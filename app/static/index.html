How it works

Printers build colors by layering cyan, magenta, yellow, and black ink. This tool renders each page, converts it to CMYK, removes the selected ink channels, and converts it back for your screen.

Good approximation, not a press proof. Paper, printer profiles, overprinting, and spot colors can make real output differ.
